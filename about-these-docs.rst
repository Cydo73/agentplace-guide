About these docs
================

This guide follows the Diataxis framework. Each page does one job, so a tutorial never turns into a reference table.

How the guide is organised
--------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 40 40

   * - Section
     - What it is for
     - Read it when
   * - Tutorial
     - Teaches you by building one working agent from start to finish.
     - You are new and want a guided first success.
   * - How to guides
     - Short steps for one specific task or problem.
     - You already have an agent and something needs fixing.
   * - Reference
     - Facts to look up: test questions, terms, sample data.
     - You need an exact answer, not a lesson.
   * - Explanation
     - Why agents behave the way they do.
     - You want to understand the cause, not only the fix.
   * - Findings
     - A report on what I tested and what it means.
     - You want evidence, results and recommendations.

The findings section is a report, not documentation, so it sits apart from the other four.

How I checked things
--------------------

Every statement about how Agentplace works comes from its public documentation, read on 2 November 2026. Those pages were last updated by Agentplace on 24 September 2026. Statements about the Tubayo site come from screenshots I took on 1 November 2026, and I mark anything I could not check.

The findings use three labels:

* **Verified**: I saw it myself, in a screenshot or on the live site.
* **Partly verified**: I saw part of it, or saw it under different conditions.
* **Unverified**: I have only the assistant's word for it.
