Patterns across the ten
-----------------------

* **Where the agent was wrong, it was usually incomplete, not invented.** It left out real listings and hedged about it.
* **Where the site assistant was wrong, it was often confident without a source.** Several answers included specifics I could not support.
* **Emergency handling differed most.** One put safety first and the other put the hotel first.
* **One long chat affected later answers.** The agent assumed I was safe in question 10 after a gas emergency in question 8. A fresh session for each question avoids that.
